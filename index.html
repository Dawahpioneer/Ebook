Who we are

About Dawah Pioneer Books

Dawah Pioneer Books started as a simple idea after creating our Youtube Channel: short, affordable ebooks that make Islamic knowledge and reflection easy to fit into a busy day — no matter your budget.

We write, translate, and design every title ourselves, blending stories, science, and reminders rooted in the Quran and Sunnah. Every book stays priced low on purpose, because we believe learning shouldn't wait for a bigger wallet.

Beyond books, part of what we build here goes back to the community — which is why a third of every order over $3 is set aside for Palestinian relief and local Mosques in need.

Transparency

Where your donation goes

One third of every order over $3 is split between two causes close to our heart.

🇵🇸

Palestinian relief

Funds are sent to trusted, established relief organizations providing food, medical aid, and emergency support to Palestinian families affected by the ongoing crisis like UNRWA (ONU) and Red Cross.

🕌

Mosques in need

The remaining share goes toward Mosques facing financial hardship — helping cover essentials like maintenance, utilities, and community programs like by giving to MATW Project (Australian Organization).

As a small, independent project, donations are currently sent out manually by our team rather than through an automated charity platform. We keep records of every transfer and are working toward publishing regular donation updates as the store grows. If you'd like proof of a specific transfer, reach out any time at contact.dawahpioneer@gmail.com.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What format do the ebooks come in?

Every ebook is delivered as a PDF, so you can read it on your phone, tablet, or computer.

How do I receive my ebook after paying?

Payment is currently handled through Interac e-Transfer (Canada only). Once we confirm your e-Transfer, we send your ebook file directly to your email — usually within a few hours.

How does the donation to Palestine and Mosques work?

For every order over $3, one third of what you pay is set aside and sent manually to Palestinian relief organizations and Mosques in need. See our "Where your donation goes" section above for more detail.

Can I get a refund?

All sales are final. Because ebooks are delivered instantly as digital files, we are unable to offer refunds once a file has been sent. If you received the wrong file or experience a technical issue, contact us and we'll make it right.

Do you ship outside Canada?

Since payment is by Interac e-Transfer, purchases are currently limited to Canada. Reach out if you're outside Canada and want to know about alternative payment options.
